ForestPlots.net Training Manual

Before you start

This manual is a practical companion to your ForestPlots training. Each chapter covers a specific step in the data workflow and is designed to be used alongside a session with a member of the ForestPlots team.

This manual currently covers:

  • Digitising Data — how to transcribe field data into the correct Excel format
  • Data Upload — how to identify and prepare the right upload format for your data
  • Query Checks — how to verify your plot dynamics after upload
  • Liana Data — the equivalent workflow for liana data (assumes familiarity with tree data)
  • Tree Data Dictionary — a reference for every column in the tree data templates
  • Common Issues & FAQs — answers to the most frequent questions

You do not need to read this manual from start to finish. Your ForestPlots contact will point you to the chapter relevant to your current task.

[!IMPORTANT] You must be trained by a member of the ForestPlots team before uploading data independently. Certain steps also require specific permissions, which you will receive after training.*

This is a living document that is updated regularly. If anything is unclear or missing, please let a member of the ForestPlots team know.
